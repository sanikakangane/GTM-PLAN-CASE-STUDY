# EventNear – Local Event Vendor Directory

EventNear is a proposed hyperlocal digital platform that connects event organisers with verified local vendors through a single, easy-to-use directory. It helps customers discover, compare and contact suitable vendors based on location, service, date, budget, pricing and availability.

## Problem

Finding reliable event vendors can be difficult because information is scattered across social media, referrals, search and offline networks. Customers often face unclear prices, repeated calls, uncertain availability and trust concerns.

## Solution

EventNear brings verified vendors, indicative price ranges, availability information and structured enquiries together in one platform.

## Key Features

- Verified local vendor profiles
- Location and service-based search
- Indicative price ranges
- Vendor ratings and reviews
- Availability information
- Vendor comparison
- Structured enquiry form
- Enquiry confirmation and vendor response flow

## Target Users

### Event Organisers

- Weddings
- Birthdays
- Engagements
- Anniversaries
- Corporate events
- College and community events

### Vendor Categories

- Decorators
- Caterers
- Photographers
- DJs
- Makeup artists
- Event planners

## Customer Workflow

**Home → Search → Vendor List → Vendor Profile → Enquiry → Confirmation**

The wider customer journey extends to:

**Need → Search → Compare → Enquire → Respond → Book → Deliver → Review**

## Business Model

EventNear follows a two-sided marketplace model:

- **Customers:** Free access
- **Vendors:** ₹799 per vendor per month
- **Revenue:** Recurring vendor subscriptions for profile visibility and customer enquiries

At 75 paying vendors, the projected monthly subscription revenue is **₹59,925**, before operating costs.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

EventNear starts with a hyperlocal pilot to build sufficient vendor density within one locality before expanding.

- Direct vendor outreach through calls, WhatsApp and in-person contact
- Partnerships with banquet halls, venues and event planners
- Social media and community channels
- Partnerships with colleges, housing societies and community organisations

## 90-Day Pilot Plan

| Period | Focus | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Build supply | 30 verified vendors |
| Days 31–60 | Test demand | 50+ customer enquiries |
| Days 61–90 | Improve & scale | 75+ vendors and 100+ enquiries |

### Pilot Success Criteria

- 75+ verified vendors
- 100+ customer enquiries
- 60%+ vendor response rate
- 20%+ enquiry-to-booking conversion
- ₹59,925 monthly subscription revenue at 75 paying vendors

## Risks & Mitigation

- **Low vendor density** → Focus acquisition on one locality first
- **Fake or unreliable vendors** → Verify vendor identity and business details
- **Outdated availability** → Encourage regular availability updates
- **Vendor churn** → Provide useful enquiries, insights and support
- **Poor service quality** → Use ratings, reviews and complaint handling

## Prototype Flow

The prototype demonstrates the core customer experience:

**Home → Search → Vendor List → Vendor Profile → Enquiry → Confirmation**

The prototype focuses on vendor discovery and enquiry submission, while booking, service delivery and reviews form part of the wider customer journey.

## Conclusion

EventNear simplifies fragmented local vendor discovery by bringing verified profiles, pricing, availability and enquiries into one hyperlocal platform. Customers can use the directory for free, while vendors pay a ₹799 monthly subscription for visibility and customer enquiries.
